Koko Tag Smart Tracking
The Challenge

Preventing the permanent loss of everyday items (keys, luggage, professional gear) without buying expensive GPS trackers, and without ever disclosing owners' personal contact details.

The Tech Solution

Web platform for generating and printing custom QR Code tags, paired with a web app and secure, anonymous messaging bridging the owner and the finder.

Usage & Results

A simple smartphone scan of the tag by whoever finds the item opens an anonymous chat to arrange its immediate return in complete confidentiality.
